Store

A public catalogue of panes and apps for your Solid pod. Panes draw your data inside the data browser; apps open it in their own tab.

How to add one
  1. Open your tool index dashboard. The Tools app finds it, or creates it, for you.
  2. Choose Browse the store.
  3. Press Add beside what you want. Its entry is copied into your own index.
